# Why Businesses Struggled with AI in 2024 — And Why 2025 Looks Promising

**Author:** Priyanshi Shah
**Published:** 2025-01-19

## Key claims

- "about 75% of companies implementing some form of generative AI by year's end"
- **Data infrastructure**: legacy systems struggled with AI requirements; data quality
  complications plagued rollouts.
- **Talent shortage**: "Companies fought over a limited pool of qualified professionals, driving
  up costs"
- **Regulatory obstacles**: the EU AI Act forced many organizations to halt initiatives while
  navigating compliance demands.
- **ROI measurement**: "scaling these successes across the enterprise proved elusive"

## Core argument

2024's struggles — data challenges, skills gaps, regulatory complexity, and unclear ROI — have
generated lessons enabling more pragmatic, sustainable AI strategies in 2025. Rather than
pursuing rapid transformation, organizations now emphasize targeted implementations with
measurable outcomes and human-AI collaboration focused on augmentation rather than replacement.
