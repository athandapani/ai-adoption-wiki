# SMB Technology Spending Statistics 2026: IT Budget Data

**Author:** Stealth Agents (no individual byline)
**Published:** 2026-06-09

## Statistics (verbatim)

- "3.4% of revenue: average SMB IT spend"
- "AI tools & automation" represents "5%" of SMB IT budget allocations
- "Gartner forecasts AI tools will represent 12-15% of SMB software spending by 2027"
- "Cloud services now consume 38% of the average SMB IT budget, up from 27% in 2022"
- "52% of SMBs raised cybersecurity budgets in 2024-2025"

## Core argument

Small business technology investment has matured into a dominant market force ($674B globally in
2024), growing faster than enterprise budgets for three consecutive years. Budget allocation has
shifted decisively toward cloud services and cybersecurity while declining from hardware, with AI
adoption accelerating rapidly. Higher-performing SMBs achieve measurable ROI within 18 months
through productivity gains and labor cost reduction, though significant waste exists in
underutilized SaaS licenses.

## Verification note

Both figures the playbook draws from this source in Section 6.4 check out: **5% of SMB IT
budgets** on AI tools, and Gartner's **12–15% of SMB software spending by 2027**.

Useful context the playbook omits: average SMB IT spend is only **3.4% of revenue**. Set against
the playbook's own 1.7%-of-revenue AI benchmark, that implies AI would be roughly *half the
entire IT budget* — which contradicts the same section's "15–35% is aligned with mid-market
practice" guidance. The playbook never reconciles these two numbers.
