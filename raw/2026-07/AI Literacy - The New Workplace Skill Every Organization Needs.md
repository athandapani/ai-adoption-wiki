# AI Literacy: The New Workplace Skill Every Organization Needs

**Author:** Victor Hoang, Co-Founder, Rework.com
**Published:** 2026

## Statistics (verbatim)

- "59% of the global workforce will require training by 2030, with AI and big data topping the
  list of needed skills, yet only 35% of organizations currently have a mature, workforce-wide
  upskilling program" (attributed to World Economic Forum / DataCamp)
- "Organizations with formal AI training programs achieve 2.3x faster AI adoption and 67% higher
  AI ROI" (attributed to OECD)
- "42% of employees say their employer expects them to learn AI on their own, while 34% report
  feeling unprepared" (attributed to DataCamp 2026)

## Core argument

AI literacy comprises **four interconnected but distinct competencies**: prompt engineering,
output verification, escalation judgment, and policy awareness. Rather than unified training,
organizations should calibrate depth by role — individual contributors focus on prompt
structuring and verification habits; managers add escalation judgment and workflow redesign;
executives add strategic investment and risk governance.

Output verification is "the least visible but most consequential gap" and requires explicit
training, because employees intuitively trust AI's confident-sounding outputs without
verification habits.
