# AI Cost Statistics 2026: What Finance, Product, & FinOps Teams Need to Know

**Author:** Lindsey Tishgart
**Published:** 2026-01-29

## Statistics (verbatim)

- "80-85% of enterprises miss their AI infrastructure forecasts by more than 25%"
- "Companies plan to spend 1.7% of revenue on AI in 2026" (up from 0.8% in 2025)
- "Less than 1% of executives report ROI of 20% or greater; 53% report only 1-5% ROI"
- "30% of GenAI projects abandoned after POC"
- "60% see minimal or no value"
- "85% of organizations increased AI investment in the prior 12 months"
- "Organizations increased AI compute spending by 166% year-over-year in Q2 2025"
- "94% of companies will continue investing even without immediate returns"
- "Companies allocating ≥25% of IT budget to AI expected to rise from 27% → 52%"
- "Companies allocating ≥50% of IT budget to AI expected to rise from 3% → 19%"
- "Nearly 40% of AI time savings are lost to rework"
- "89% of organizations haven't updated job roles for AI"

## Core argument

Traditional IT planning and forecasting models fail with AI workloads. Organizations must
establish dedicated AI financial governance — specialized cost taxonomies, outcome-based
metrics, joint finance-technology ownership, and workflow redesign — to move from pilot
abandonment and minimal ROI toward the 5% achieving significant returns.
