# AI Spending Forecast: Turning Numbers Into Power

**Author:** MyBusinessFuture editorial team (no individual byline)
**Published:** 2026-04-25

## Gartner figures (verbatim)

- "global AI spending forecast to $2.52 trillion for 2026 (up 44 percent year-over-year)"
- "$6.31 trillion in total IT spending with 13.5 percent growth"
- "Software spending alone: $1.44 trillion"
- "Datacenter systems: $788 billion"
- Software spending growth: "15.1 percent"

## Core argument

The Gartner figures represent actionable negotiation leverage rather than predictive
forecasting. Mid-market organizations must immediately audit software contracts, reserve GPU
capacity with hyperscalers on 18-month commitments, and build internal AI talent strategies
across three dimensions (career paths, training programs, work modes) to avoid competitive
disadvantage by 2027 and capture learning effects that become difficult for laggards to close.
