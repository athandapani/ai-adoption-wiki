# Turning Institutional Knowledge into Strategic AI Assets: A Guide for Digital Leaders

**Author:** Mayank Kukreja, Technical Architect ("16+ years of expertise")
**Published:** updated 2026-05-26

## Description

Explains how RAG bridges the gap between enterprise AI systems and organizational knowledge,
enabling teams to access accurate, context-grounded answers from internal documents.

## Statistics (verbatim)

- "Organizations that have implemented RAG-powered internal systems report meaningful reductions
  in query resolution time, often in the range of 40 to 60 percent"

## Core argument

RAG doesn't require rebuilding institutional knowledge — it **activates existing documents**
already housed in organizational systems (SharePoint, Confluence, archives) by connecting them to
AI models. This unlocks measurable business value through faster sales cycles, reduced support
costs, accelerated onboarding, and improved compliance outcomes. Success depends on content
governance, retrieval precision, and auditability rather than engineering sophistication alone.
