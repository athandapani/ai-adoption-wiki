# AI use cases: an in-depth trend overview for 2026

**Author:** Itransition (no individual byline)
**Published:** 2025-12-23

## Statistics (verbatim)

- "70% of transportation and logistics companies surveyed reported adopting AI solutions"
- "Top AI use cases in manufacturing by adoption include production (39%), inventory management
  (33%), and quality operations (24%)"
- "AI can help manufacturing companies reduce failures in the assembly process by 70%, cut
  quality control efforts by 50%, and increase visual inspection accuracy by 80%"
- "88% in 2025" — organizations using AI tools
- GenAI deployment "growing from 20% in 2024 to 36% in 2025"
- Customer service (57%), marketing/sales (54%) lead functional adoption
- Administrative automation generates "27-30% cost reductions"

## Core argument

Organizations have transitioned from AI experimentation to scaled implementation, with adoption
expanding across diverse business functions. Strategic AI investments yield greatest revenue
gains in finance and marketing, while administrative automation generates 27–30% cost reductions.
