# Indian CEOs are doubling down on AI investments; earmarking 1.7% of revenue for AI in 2026: BCG report

**Author:** Debangana Ghosh
**Published:** 2026-01-15, 14:56 IST
**Underlying source:** BCG **AI Radar 2026**

## Statistics (verbatim)

- "Corporate AI investment is expected to double in 2026 to about 1.7 percent of revenues, more
  than twice the increase seen in 2025"
- "88 percent of Indian leaders expect AI to generate positive return on investment (ROI),
  outpacing the global average of 82 percent"
- "Only 36% of India's workforce is skilled in AI, below the 44% global average"
- "We figured that 21 percent companies in India (out of 200 firms surveyed) were looking at
  spending more than $50 million in AI investments" — Nipun Kalra, India Leader – BCG X
- Survey base: 200 firms in India

## Core argument

Indian corporates are more optimistic about AI returns than global peers and are doubling down on
investment despite near-term uncertainty, viewing AI as a long-term strategic priority rather than
discretionary technology spend.

## ⚠ The report's real name

The playbook's Section 6.1 and Appendix B call this "**BCG Radar 6**" and "BCG's 2026 AI
Investment Survey". This source names it **"AI Radar 2026"**. "BCG Radar 6" does not appear here,
in ref [^4], or in ref [^31] — all three of which report the same 1.7% figure. The playbook's
Appendix B recommends ingesting "BCG Radar 6: AI Investments Surge, CEOs Take the Lead (2026)" as
a primary source; that title appears to be an invention.

## Scope caveat

This is **India-specific coverage** (200 Indian firms) of a global BCG report. The playbook cites
it in Section 6.1 to support a general "companies plan to spend approximately 1.7% of annual
revenues" benchmark, and to source the sector split (tech/financial ~2%, industrial/real estate
~0.8%). **Those sector figures do not appear in this capture.**
