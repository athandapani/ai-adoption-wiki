# Privacy and AI Trends Report 2026: Shadow AI Emerges as a Growing Threat While Core Privacy Challenges Persist

**Issuer:** DataGrail (press release via GlobeNewswire)
**Published:** 2026-05-27

## Statistics (verbatim)

- "63.6% of AI-powered vendors did not disclose third-party AI subprocessors in their legal
  documentation"
- "32.8% of AI systems also participate in at least one high-risk activity"
- "63% of websites still fail to honor GPC and other universal opt-out mechanisms"
- "DSR deletion requests have surged 567% since 2021"
- "87% of all DSRs are deletion requests"
- "$1.5 million per year" — estimated cost for mid-sized organizations managing requests manually

## Core argument

Privacy programs must adopt AI-driven automation tools to address escalating regulatory
complexity, rising data subject requests, and undisclosed AI subprocessor risks that traditional
compliance approaches cannot adequately manage.
