# AI Literacy Training Explained: What Every Non-Technical Employee Needs to Know

**Author:** AI Skills Team
**Published:** 2026-05-14

## Description

Organizations should prioritize foundational AI literacy for all employees before implementing AI
tools, ensuring staff understand capabilities, limitations, and safe usage practices.

## Recommended curriculum

- **Duration:** 4–8 hours of contact time across 2–4 sessions
- **Format:** live cohorts of 15–25 participants
- **Topics:**
  - Foundations of generative AI (non-mathematical explanation)
  - Practical prompting techniques
  - "Hallucinations, bias, data protection, intellectual property, and your organisation's
    specific acceptable use policy"
  - Hands-on practice with real work tasks
  - Use-case mapping (3–5 job-specific applications per learner)

## Claims

The article describes a common post-rollout pattern when literacy training is skipped:
"15% of staff use it daily, 30% have logged in once, and the rest haven't touched it".
