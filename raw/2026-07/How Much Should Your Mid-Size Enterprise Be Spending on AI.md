# How Much Should Your Mid-Size Enterprise Be Spending on AI?

**Author:** Bob Marsh
**Published:** ~2026-06 (LinkedIn reported "1 month ago" at capture time)

## Post text

"How much should your mid-size enterprise be spending on AI? Recent research from BCG shows
they're planning to spend roughly 1.7% of annual revenues on AI in 2026, more than double what
they spent in 2025. And 94% say they'll continue at that level or higher even if this year's
initiatives don't deliver a return. That level of commitment is striking when you set it next to
the other number in the same report: meaningful bottom-line impact is showing up for about 6% of
the companies currently using AI. The tools are more capable than they've ever been, so the
bottom line impact gap isn't coming from a technology failure. It's coming more from effective
planning, goal setting, and how ready organizations actually are to absorb change at the pace AI
requires. When an AI initiative stalls, it almost never stalls because the LLM model
underperformed. It stalls because the people who were supposed to change how they work didn't
change. Because the data feeding the system wasn't trustworthy enough for people to act on.
Because the infrastructure created constraints that weren't fully planned for. Because the tool
added friction or confusion instead of removing it, and people quietly went back to what they
knew. None of those failure points shows up in a standard effort / impact analysis before the
project starts. They show up after going live, when it's radically more expensive to fix. The
organizations closing the gap between AI investment and AI return are asking a different question
before they build anything. Not just whether they can implement the technology, but whether the
organization is set up to actually absorb it."
