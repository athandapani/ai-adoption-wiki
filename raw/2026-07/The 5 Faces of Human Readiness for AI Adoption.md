# The 5 faces of human readiness for AI adoption – and how to work with them

**Authors:** David Timis; Shaista Khilji, Professor of Human and Organizational Learning &
International Affairs, The George Washington University
**Published:** 2026-06-01
**Part of:** WEF Centre for AI Excellence

## Method

"a two-phase study combining sentiment analysis of public social media spaces with in-depth
interviews across multiple industries"

## The five archetypes (verbatim names)

1. **AI enthusiasts** — "Driven by innovation, competitive advantage and efficiency, these
   individuals are eager to champion new tools." Channel them as "AI champions" or internal
   super-users.
2. **AI curious** — "Intrigued by the technology's potential but fundamentally pragmatic, they
   withhold full buy-in until their technical and operational questions are answered." Bridge with
   low-stakes "sandbox" hackathons.
3. **AI cautious** — "Acutely aware of data security, ethical guardrails and the unintended
   consequences AI might have on human collaboration." Address with mandated human-in-the-loop
   protocols.
4. **AI sceptics** — "Intellectual pragmatists who doubt corporate hype and demand empirical
   proof of utility before altering their workflows." Address with transparent A/B testing.
5. **AI opposed** — "Driven by deep psychological or socio-economic fears, such as immediate job
   displacement or corporate surveillance, these individuals represent the deepest layer of
   resistance." Address underlying anxieties directly, e.g. a formal "Pro-Human AI Charter".

## Frontstage compliance, backstage resistance

The paper's central insight, and one the playbook omits entirely:

> "In frontstage settings – public, visible areas – employees may appear to comply with AI
> mandates to manage impressions. However, in backstage settings, they often engage in informal
> critique, 'clowning' AI tools, or finding ways to circumvent and delay their use."

> "Even 'AI enthusiasts' tasked with implementing strategies related to this emerging technology
> often privately doubt their effectiveness."

## Other statistics (verbatim)

- "In the United States alone, 62% of adults now interact with AI several times a week."
- Global private AI investment "estimated at $660 billion in 2026"
- The gap between what AI can do and how it's used is a "capability overhang"

## Three recommendations

1. "Prioritize cultural change over technical solutions" — radical organizational transparency
2. "Focus on 'pro-worker' AI and human-machine symbiosis" — ask "What systemic problem are we
   trying to solve?" not "How can we use AI?"
3. "Address the roots of employee unease" — including energy costs, human connection, erosion of
   core cognitive skills

## ⚠ The playbook renamed every archetype, and the taxonomy doesn't survive it

Playbook Section 8.4 presents "five distinct employee postures" citing this source. **All five
names are changed, and two are substantively different concepts:**

| WEF (actual) | Playbook | Faithful? |
|---|---|---|
| AI enthusiasts | Enthusiasts | ✅ |
| AI curious | **Pragmatists** | ~ renamed; sense roughly preserved |
| AI cautious — *data security, ethics, collaboration harm* | **Skeptics** — *"Doubt AI's reliability"* | ❌ conflated |
| AI sceptics — *demand empirical proof* | **Anxious** — *"Fear job displacement"* | ❌ wrong concept |
| AI opposed — *job displacement, surveillance fears* | **Resisters** — *"Active opposition"* | ~ partial |

The playbook's "Anxious" (job-displacement fear) is the WEF's **AI opposed**, not its sceptics.
Its "Skeptics" (doubt reliability) is closer to the WEF's **sceptics** than to "cautious". The
five-way mapping is scrambled, so the playbook's per-posture interventions are attached to the
wrong archetypes.

It also cites this as "World Economic Forum research (May 2026)" — published **1 June 2026**.

And it drops **frontstage/backstage resistance**, which is the actual finding of the study and
the one most relevant to the playbook's own shadow-AI section: employees who publicly comply and
privately circumvent are precisely the shadow-AI population.
