# AI Skills Gap Widens as Training Lags Behind

**Author:** mediastack
**Published:** 2026-04-10
**Underlying source:** Forrester Research

## Forrester findings (verbatim)

- "only half offer training to non-technical employees, with that figure growing just 4
  percentage points between 2024 and 2025"
- "43 percent of employees believing many people will lose jobs to automation within five years"

## Core argument

Organizations must treat AI literacy as a strategic priority by implementing continuous,
hands-on learning programs and improving communication about AI to maximize productivity and
ROI, rather than deploying AI tools without corresponding workforce preparation.
