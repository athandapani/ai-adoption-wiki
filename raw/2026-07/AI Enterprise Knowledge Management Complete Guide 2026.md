## Verification note

The playbook's Section 10.1 pricing table reproduces this source accurately across all seven
tools.

**But note a contradiction the playbook silently inherits:** this source says knowledge workers
spend **"1.8 hours/day"** locating information. The playbook's Section 2.4 says **"2.5 hours
daily"** (ref 10, Stratechi) and builds its $1M–$2M annual recovered-capacity estimate on the
larger figure. Both sources are cited in the playbook; only the bigger number is used. The
difference is ~39%, and it flows straight through to the headline ROI claim.
