# How Much Will Your AI Implementation Cost? SaaS vs Custom vs API-First in 2026

**Author:** Krunal Panchal
**Published:** 2026-03-24

## Cost figures (verbatim)

**SaaS AI:**
- "$75–$330/user/mo" (Salesforce Einstein)
- "$890–$3,600/mo" (HubSpot AI)
- "$0.99 per resolution" (Intercom Fin)
- "Year 1 cost exceeds $163,000" (50-person company example)

**Custom AI:**
- "$360,000–$900,000+" (AI Engineering, 12 months)
- "$473,400–$1,870,000+" (Year 1 Total across tiers)
- "Model drift monitoring infrastructure costs $2,000–$8,000/month"
- "$25,000–$60,000/month" (vector DB at enterprise scale)

**API-First:**
- "$45,000–$90,000" (Year 1 startup)
- "$115,000–$360,000" (Year 1 mid-market)
- "$420,000–$1,400,000" (Year 1 enterprise)
- "3-Year Total (mid-market): $150,000–$900,000"

**Hidden costs:**
- "47% of AI budgets" exceed estimates (Gartner 2025)
- "Budget overruns: $387,000" (financial services case study)

## Core argument

Organizations systematically underestimate AI deployment costs by excluding recurring
operational expenses — model monitoring, prompt versioning, vector database hosting, compliance
audits, and inference scaling.

The three models trade upfront investment against long-term flexibility: SaaS minimizes
engineering but locks in vendor dependency; custom maximizes control but demands significant
infrastructure overhead; **API-first balances speed with iterability for most mid-market
scenarios**. Accurate budgeting requires explicit line items for drift monitoring, data
pipelines, and compliance work — "typically 40% contingency above initial engineering estimates".
