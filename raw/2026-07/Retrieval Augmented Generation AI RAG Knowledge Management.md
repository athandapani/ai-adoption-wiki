# Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): Transform Enterprise Knowledge | 300-500% ROI

**Author:** Stratechi.com (no individual byline)
**Published:** 2026

## ROI and productivity metrics (verbatim)

- "enterprise-wide RAG implementation typically delivers a 300-500% return on investment within
  the first year alone"
- Knowledge workers spend approximately "2.5 hours daily" searching for information
- "productivity gains of 30-50%", recovering roughly "45-75 minutes per employee daily"
- For enterprises with 1,000 knowledge workers: "$5-8 million annually in recovered productive
  time"
- Decision quality: RAG reduces information gaps by "40-60% for critical decisions"
- Onboarding: new employee time-to-productivity improves by "30-40% faster"
- Knowledge preservation: organizations retain approximately "70% of previously ephemeral
  knowledge" from departing senior employees
- Compliance: reduces compliance risk exposure by "25-35%"

## Core argument

These quantifiable benefits represent "only the foundation" of RAG's transformative potential for
organizational competitiveness.
