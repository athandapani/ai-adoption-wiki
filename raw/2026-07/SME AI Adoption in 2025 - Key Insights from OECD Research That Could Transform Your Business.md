# SME AI Adoption in 2025: Key Insights from OECD Research That Could Transform Your Business

**Author:** Daijobu AI (team-attributed, no individual byline)
**Published:** 2025-05-14

## Description

Analysis of OECD research on artificial intelligence adoption among small and medium-sized
enterprises, identifying adoption rates, barriers, and security vulnerabilities.

## Statistics (verbatim)

Adoption:
- "39% of SMEs now use AI applications (up from 26% in 2024)"
- "26% specifically use generative AI (up from 18% last year)"
- "17% recognize they make passive use of AI through platforms they already use"

Depth of integration:
- "Only 8% of businesses have reached 'transformative' digital integration"
- "16% remain at basic digital adoption levels"

Barriers:
- "Maintenance costs (40%)" — top barrier
- "Lack of time for training (39%)"
- "Hardware costs (32%)"
- "27% reported that 'available supports were not adapted to their needs'"
- "Only 21% of SMEs are even aware of government support programs"

Security:
- "72% have inadequate digital security measures"
- "32% of SMEs experienced a security breach in the past year"
- "9% of businesses have no digital security measures at all"
- "63% have only basic or intermediate protections"
- "Only 12% conduct regular cybersecurity assessments with experts"

Outcomes among generative-AI users:
- "91% reported significant productivity improvements"
- "76% experienced enhanced innovation capabilities"

Macro:
- "AI will contribute €15.7 trillion to the global economy by 2030"

## Core argument

SMEs face significant barriers to effective AI adoption despite growing interest, primarily due
to costs and security gaps. Custom, tailored solutions addressing these specific pain points are
more effective than generic off-the-shelf products.
