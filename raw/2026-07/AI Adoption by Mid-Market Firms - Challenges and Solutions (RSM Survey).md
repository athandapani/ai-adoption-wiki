# AI Adoption by Mid-Market Firms: Challenges and Solutions (RSM 2025 Survey)

**Author:** Brandon Braganza
**Published:** 2025
**Underlying source:** RSM's 2025 Middle Market AI Survey (US and Canada)

## Key findings (verbatim)

Adoption:
- "78-92% of mid-sized firms are utilizing AI"
- Similar proportions are experimenting with generative AI
- "89% plan to increase AI budgets, with 74% focusing on generative AI"

Performance vs. expectations:
- "85% report AI has exceeded their expectations"
- "54% found deployment harder than anticipated"

Support needs:
- "67% of organizations acknowledge requiring external assistance for strategy and governance"

## Common applications

Quality control, customer service, repetitive task automation, productivity improvements.

## RSM's own investment

"$1 billion over three years" on AI agents, infrastructure, talent, and client-facing tools;
efficiency gains "reaching 80% in compliance and audit functions".

## Core takeaway

"Mid-market firms are actively investing in AI and seeing results, but struggle with scaling. To
succeed, blend internal adoption with external support — and back it with strategic investment."
