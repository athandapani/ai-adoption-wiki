# Shadow AI — The Hidden Risk in Your Enterprise (2026 Update)

**Author:** Abhishek G Sharma
**Published:** 2026-01-11

**Important:** this is a **secondary** source. Its figures come from the Netskope 2026 Cloud and
Threat Report and the IBM 2025 Cost of a Data Breach Report. The playbook cites this post rather
than either primary source.

## Netskope 2026 Cloud and Threat Report (verbatim)

- "Average org activity: ~18k prompts/month across GenAI tools (up ~6× yr-over-yr)"
- "Policy violations: ~223 GenAI-related data policy violations per month on average"
- "Personal instances: ~47% of GenAI users access tools via personal/unmanaged accounts"

## Data exposure breakdown

- Source code: 42% of violations
- Regulated data (personal/financial/health): 32%
- Intellectual property: 16%

## IBM 2025 Cost of a Data Breach Report (verbatim)

- "20% of organizations reported a breach due to security incidents involving Shadow AI"
- "High Shadow AI environments saw +$670,000 higher breach costs vs low/no Shadow AI"
- "Shadow AI incidents resulted in more PII (65%) and more IP (40%) being compromised"

## 3-step governance framework

1. **DISCOVER** (weeks 1–2): network analysis, browser audits, employee surveys, CASB tools
2. **CLASSIFY** (weeks 3–4): risk-based assessment matrix (Critical / High / Medium / Low)
3. **GOVERN** (weeks 5–12): policy, training, DLP controls, monitoring, audit evidence

## Verification note

Every shadow-AI figure in the playbook's Section 4.5 checks out against this capture: 18k
prompts, 223 violations, 47% personal accounts, +$670K breach cost, 65% PII / 40% IP, and the
42/32/16 data-type split. This is the best-sourced section of the playbook.
