# Overcoming AI Resistance: Change Management Strategies That Actually Work

**Author:** Eric Lundberg (written); Linus Ingemarsson (reviewed)
**Published:** 2026-05-23

## Statistics (verbatim)

- "82% of AI adoption failures trace to change management gaps, not technology"
- "18% of firms currently use AI in at least one business function (Jan 2026)"
- "83% of Agile practitioners use AI, but most spend under 10% of work time with it"

## McKinsey finding (verbatim)

"#1 barrier to AI adoption is organizational — change management and silos outrank technology
gaps"

## Academy of Management study

Supervisors who co-present AI-generated feedback alongside employees — rather than delegating AI
interactions entirely — demonstrably reduce employee pushback to AI outputs.

## Core argument

Resistance stems primarily from psychological, structural, and informational gaps — not
technology inadequacy. Organizations must diagnose resistance pre-deployment through stakeholder
mapping, anonymous surveys, and workflow analysis. Tailored communication (prevention-focused vs.
promotion-focused language), visible leadership modeling, and iterative pilot sequencing —
starting with invisible-workflow AI tools — are more impactful than generic training programs.
