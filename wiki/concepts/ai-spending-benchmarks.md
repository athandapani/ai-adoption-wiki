## Body

**What holds up.** BCG's **1.7% of annual revenue** on AI in 2026 is confirmed by three independent
captures — refs [^4], [^28], and [^31] — and ref [^31] supplies the 2025 base (**0.8%**), which
makes the playbook's "more than double" check out exactly. This is solid.

Applied to the band:

| Revenue | @ 1.7% |
|---|---|
| $50M | ~$850K/yr |
| $100M | ~$1.7M/yr |
| $200M | ~$3.4M/yr |
| $500M | ~$8.5M/yr |

**Actual SMB spend** (Creative Genius, 312 firms — solid methodology):

| Revenue | Median | Top quartile |
|---|---|---|
| $5M–$25M | $34,000 | $112,000 |
| $25M–$100M | $148,000 | $420,000 |
| $100M–$200M | $390,000 | $1.2M |

Note the gap: 1.7% of $100M is $1.7M; the *actual* median at $100M–$200M is **$390,000**. The
benchmark and the observed behavior differ by ~4×. Spend also scales **sub-linearly** with revenue
— the reverse of the cloud pattern.

**IT budget share** (refs [^31] [^32]): firms allocating ≥25% of IT budget to AI expected to rise
from **27% → 52%**. AI is ~**5% of SMB IT budgets** now, forecast by Gartner to reach **12–15% of
SMB software spending by 2027**.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**The sector splits are unverified.** "Technology and financial institutions lead at ~2%;
industrial and real estate trail at ~0.8%" is cited to [^28][^29]. Ref [^29] was unreachable; ref
[^28] doesn't contain it. Unsupported.

**Four of five per-employee figures are wrong.** Section 6.5 cites AI Stack Hub for technology
(~$5,200 — actually **$11,200**), manufacturing (~$1,800 — actually **$900**), healthcare (~$2,800
— actually **$4,200**), and logistics (~$2,100 — **absent from the source**). Only professional
services ($3,470) matches. The section's headline "$360K–$700K per year" recommendation for a
200-employee traditional-industry firm is derived from the two worst figures, so it doesn't follow.

**The report title is invented.** "BCG Radar 6" appears in none of the three captured sources. The
report is **AI Radar 2026**.

**An internal contradiction nobody reconciles.** Ref [^32] says average SMB IT spend is **3.4% of
revenue**. If AI is 1.7% of revenue, AI is *half the entire IT budget* — which contradicts the same
section's "15–35% of IT budget is aligned with mid-market practice". Both figures are in the
playbook; the arithmetic between them isn't.
