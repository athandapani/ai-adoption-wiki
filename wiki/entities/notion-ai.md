## Body

**Category:** Workspace + AI.
**Price:** Business $20 + AI $10/user/mo; Enterprise $25 + AI $10/user/mo.
**Differentiator:** "Workspace + AI Q&A + AI Connectors" — native to a leading workspace, so
migration friction is near zero.
**Best fit:** Notion-native teams, startups through mid-market.

Notion appears twice in the playbook, in different roles. As a knowledge tool it anchors the
$50M–$150M recommended stack. As a **governance** tool it's the Basic tier of
[[three-tier-sme-governance|the three-tier framework]] — "Notion + Google Sheets, $0/month" for the
risk register, tool log, and AUP — and the [[ninety-day-governance-launch|90-day plan]] stands
governance up in it during days 31–60.

That dual role is the practical case for it in a lean org: one tool the team already knows, doing
both jobs, with no procurement cycle.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

The $10/user/mo AI add-on is the line item that surprises people. At 200 employees that's
$24K/year on top of $48K/year of seats — $72K/year for a mid-market firm whose *entire* median AI
spend at $100M–$200M revenue is $390K.

Using Notion as the governance system of record has a real weakness the playbook doesn't flag:
[[three-tier-sme-governance|the Standard tier]] requires a 3-year audit trail. Notion is a
collaborative workspace with page history, not an audit log — anyone with edit access can change a
risk register entry, and reconstructing who-knew-what-when for a regulator is not what the tool is
for.
