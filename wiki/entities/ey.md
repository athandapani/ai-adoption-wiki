## Body

Playbook Section 6.4 attributes to **"EY's AI-Driven Productivity and Investment Survey (December
2025)"** the finding that companies allocating **≥25% of IT budget to AI** were expected to rise
from **27% to 52%** within two years.

**The figure is independently confirmed** — Lindsey Tishgart (ref [^31]) reports exactly "Companies
allocating ≥25% of IT budget to AI expected to rise from 27% → 52%", and adds a second tier the
playbook omits: those allocating ≥50% rising from 3% → 19%.

So the number is real. The attribution is the problem.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**EY has no numbered reference in the playbook.** Section 6.4 cites [^14][^31] — Gartner coverage
and Mavvrik — while naming EY in the prose. Neither captured source mentions EY. The survey has no
URL anywhere in the document, and unlike OECD, BCG, Prosci, and Vectara, EY isn't even in Appendix
B's recommended ingestion queue.

The named survey — "EY AI-Driven Productivity and Investment Survey, December 2025" — may well
exist and be the ultimate origin of the 27%→52% figure that Mavvrik carries. But nothing in the
playbook's reference list connects them, so the naming is an assertion.

Status `revisiting`: the statistic is corroborated; the entity's role in producing it is not.
