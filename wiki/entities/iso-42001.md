## Body

ISO/IEC 42001 is the AI Management System standard — the ISO-family governance framework for
organizations building or deploying AI, structurally analogous to ISO 27001 for information
security.

Its role in this vault is as the spine of [[three-tier-sme-governance|the tiered framework]]:

| Tier | ISO 42001 coverage | Time | Cost/mo |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | EU AI Act Annex A only | 2 weeks | $0 |
| Standard | **"ISO 42001 Lite"** | 4 weeks | ~$200 |
| Premium | Full ISO 42001, full audit trail | 8+ weeks | $1,500+ |

**Audit trail retention: 3 years minimum.**

For most mid-market firms in the $50M–$200M range, the Standard tier — "ISO 42001 Lite" — is the
recommended starting point.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**"ISO 42001 Lite" is not a thing.** It's ref [^23]'s coinage for a subset of the standard's
controls, not a recognized conformance level. A firm implementing it can say it follows ISO
42001-derived practices; it cannot claim conformance or certification. The playbook's tier table
presents it in a "Compliance Coverage" column, which invites exactly the wrong inference.

**The standard itself was never read.** ISO 42001 is paywalled (ISO standards are sold, not
published), has no numbered reference in the playbook, and everything here arrives via ref [^23] —
a July 2025 blog post by an author credited as "admin". Appendix B recommends ingesting the
standard as a "Foundational governance framework"; it wasn't, and at ISO's pricing it largely
can't be, casually.

**Timeline realism:** 8+ weeks to full ISO 42001 is implausible for an organization with no
compliance function. ISO 27001 certification typically runs 6–12 months for a mid-market firm.
Nothing suggests 42001 is faster.
