## Body

McKinsey's April 2026 analysis, as the playbook's Section 8.1 puts it, identifies **change
management and organizational silos** as the top barriers to AI adoption — ahead of technology
gaps, regulatory concerns, and data quality. Both are leadership failures, not technical ones.

Via ref [^35]: "#1 barrier to AI adoption is organizational — change management and silos outrank
technology gaps".

This is the direct counterweight to [[oecd|OECD]]'s finding that management and employee
resistance rank *last* among barriers at 7% and 6%. The playbook asserts both, in Sections 1.3 and
8.1, and never reconciles them. The likely resolution is that they measure different lifecycle
stages — barriers to starting vs. causes of failing.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**McKinsey is never read directly.** The April 2026 analysis has no URL, no title, and no numbered
reference in the playbook — it arrives only through ref [^35], a vendor insights page that
paraphrases it in a single sentence. Appendix B recommends ingesting "McKinsey AI Adoption Analysis
(April 2026)" as a primary source; it was not.

So the playbook's entire Section 8 framing rests on a one-line second-hand paraphrase of an
uncited document. The claim is plausible and widely echoed — but as sourced here, it is not
verifiable.
