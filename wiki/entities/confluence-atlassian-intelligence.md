## Body

**Category:** Wiki + AI.
**Price:** $5.75–11/user + AI add-on.
**Differentiator:** deep Jira/DevOps integration via Atlassian Intelligence.
**Best fit:** Atlassian-centric or engineering-heavy organizations.

The playbook's Section 10.2 recommends it for the top of the band — **$200M–$500M ARR with
traditional/legacy IT**: "Confluence AI + custom RAG on top of ERP/SAP data, ~$200–500K/year",
aimed at manufacturing, distribution, and regulated industries with an SAP or Oracle core.

That's the one recommended stack that matches the
[[three-layer-reference-architecture|reference architecture]] — a wiki surface over a custom
retrieval layer sitting on ERP exports, which is Layer 2 as drawn.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

The recommendation contains the hardest engineering work in the playbook, stated as a phrase:
**"custom RAG on top of ERP/SAP data"**. SAP's data model is not a document corpus. Extracting,
structuring, and grounding against it is a data engineering programme — precisely the capability
[[why-mid-market-is-structurally-different|the playbook says mid-market doesn't have]]. The
$200–500K/year figure is unsourced and almost certainly excludes it.

The cheapest per-seat price in the table is also misleading for the segment it's recommended to.
Confluence is priced for engineering orgs; the traditional-industry firms this stack targets are
the least likely to already run Atlassian, so "cheap per seat" arrives with a migration.
