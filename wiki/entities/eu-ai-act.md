## Body

The enforcement timeline, verbatim from ref [^22]:

> "Prohibited practices took effect in **February 2025**, and high-risk system requirements became
> enforceable in **August 2026**, carrying fines of up to **€35 million or 7% of global annual
> revenue**."

The Act's Annex A is the compliance target for the Basic tier of
[[three-tier-sme-governance|the governance framework]] — 2 weeks, $0/month, Notion + Google Sheets.

Ref [^13] records the second-order effect: the Act "forced many organizations to halt initiatives
while navigating compliance demands" during 2024.

**The penalty is the fact that should drive mid-market attention.** 7% of global annual revenue for
a $200M firm is $14M — an order of magnitude beyond any AI budget the playbook contemplates, and
far beyond the $47,200 average penalty ref [^23] uses to justify tiered governance.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**The playbook understates this considerably.** Section 4.1 says only that high-risk requirements
are "phasing in through 2026" — no August date, no €35M, no 7%. Both figures are in ref [^22],
which it cites. The single most board-relevant fact about AI regulation is in a source the playbook
read and didn't use.

The counterweight worth holding: **most mid-market AI use is probably not high-risk under the Act.**
Internal knowledge search, meeting summarization, and drafting fall largely outside Annex III.
Employment screening, credit decisioning, and critical infrastructure don't. The exposure depends
entirely on use case, not on company size — and neither the playbook nor any source here provides a
mid-market firm with a way to determine which of its AI systems are in scope.

That gap matters more than the timeline. A firm that reads this section and buys ISO 42001 tooling
for a Copilot deployment has bought compliance theatre; a firm using AI in hiring and reading the
same section has no idea it's in Annex III.

Read via a security vendor's blog, not the regulation. The Act is public — this is a verifiable
claim that wasn't verified.
