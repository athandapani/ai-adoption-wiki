## Body

Forte's *Building a Second Brain* is the personal knowledge management (PKM) methodology the
playbook cites as one of the two converging traditions behind the enterprise Digital Second Brain.
Its sequence: **capture, organize, distill, express**.

The playbook mentions it once, in Section 2.1, and never again. Everything after that is Karpathy.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**Uncited.** Forte has no numbered reference, no URL, and no entry in Appendix B's ingestion queue.
He is named once and dropped.

Status is `dormant` because that's what this node honestly is in the corpus: a historical
attribution rather than a live influence. The playbook's architecture inherits nothing traceable
from Forte — no capture-organize-distill-express structure appears in the
[[three-layer-reference-architecture|reference architecture]], the folder layout, or the
compilation prompt.

The omission has a cost, though. Forte's method is explicitly about **distillation** — progressively
summarizing so future-you can act on it. That's the maintenance discipline the Karpathy pipeline
gestures at with "linting" and leaves unspecified, and it's the weakest layer in every
implementation described in this vault. The tradition the playbook name-checks and discards is the
one with an answer to its hardest problem.

PKM methods are also designed for **one person's** knowledge, with one person's context and one
person's judgment about what matters. Nothing in Forte's work addresses multi-author knowledge,
permissions, or contested facts — the defining problems of enterprise knowledge.
