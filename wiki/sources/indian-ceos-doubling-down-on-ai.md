## Body

**Underlying source: BCG AI Radar 2026.**

- "Corporate AI investment is expected to double in 2026 to about **1.7 percent of revenues**, more
  than twice the increase seen in 2025"
- "**88 percent of Indian leaders** expect AI to generate positive ROI, outpacing the global average
  of **82 percent**"
- "Only **36%** of India's workforce is skilled in AI, below the **44% global average**"
- "21 percent [of] companies in India (out of 200 firms surveyed) were looking at spending more than
  $50 million in AI investments" — Nipun Kalra, India Leader, BCG X

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**This capture names the report: "AI Radar 2026".** The playbook calls it **"BCG Radar 6: AI
Investments Surge, CEOs Take the Lead (2026)"** in Section 6.1 and recommends ingesting it under
that title in Appendix B. "BCG Radar 6" appears in none of the three sources reporting its findings.
The title appears to be invented.

**Scope caveat the playbook ignores:** this is **India-specific** coverage — 200 Indian firms — used
in Section 6.1 to support a general mid-market benchmark. The 1.7% figure does appear to be BCG's
global number (refs [^4] and [^31] agree), so the benchmark survives. But the **sector split** the
playbook cites to [^28][^29] — tech/financial ~2%, industrial/real estate ~0.8% — **is not in this
capture**, and ref [^29] was unreachable. Unverified.

The 82% global / 88% India ROI-expectation figures also sit oddly beside BCG's own 6%-see-impact
finding from the same report family: expectation and realization differ by an order of magnitude.
