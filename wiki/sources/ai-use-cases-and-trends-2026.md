## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**The playbook's manufacturing headline is not in this source.** Section 7.2 claims predictive
maintenance delivers a "documented **20–40% reduction in unplanned downtime**", citing this
reference. That figure does not appear. This source's manufacturing numbers are entirely different —
70% assembly-failure reduction, 50% QC-effort cut, 80% visual-inspection accuracy — and none is
about downtime.

The "88% in 2025" adoption figure adds a fifth incompatible number to this vault's collection: 18%
(ref [^35]), 39% (ref [^1]), 75% (ref [^13]), 78–92% (ref [^2]), 88% here. Every source measures
"using AI" differently and none says how.

Itransition is a software development company; the piece is marketing content with vendor-neutral
framing. Published December 2025 as a 2026 forecast.
