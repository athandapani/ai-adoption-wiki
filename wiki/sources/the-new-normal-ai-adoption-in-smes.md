## Body

**Authors:** Julia Schwaeke, Anna Peters, Dominik K. Kanbach, Sascha Kraus, Paul Jones.
**Journal:** Journal of Small Business Management.

The only peer-reviewed source in the playbook's 43 references.

From the abstract: a "systematic literature review wherein we analyze **106 peer-reviewed
articles** on AI adoption in SMEs and categorize states and trends into **eight clusters**: (1)
compatibility, (2) infrastructure, (3) knowledge, (4) resources, (5) culture, (6) competition, (7)
regulation, and (8) ecosystem: according to the **technology–organization–environment model**."

Identified gaps in the literature: "overlooking trends identification as a pivotal driver and
neglecting legal requirements."

It reports **no primary survey** and produces **no original adoption statistics**. It is a synthesis
of prior work.
