## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**A July 2025 blog post credited to "admin", cited in a 2026 playbook as current governance
guidance.** That matters concretely: the [[eu-ai-act|EU AI Act]]'s high-risk requirements became
enforceable in **August 2026** — after this was written. A Basic tier scoped to "EU AI Act Annex A"
was designed against a regulatory landscape that has since moved.

**The playbook silently extends the table**, appending "Acceptable Use Policy" to Basic and "shadow
AI controls" to Standard. Neither is in the source.

**Tiers are defined by tooling and cost, not risk.** Tier selection should follow a risk
assessment; here it follows company size. A 60-person firm running customer-facing AI on regulated
data needs more than a 400-person firm using Copilot for drafting.

"ISO 42001 Lite" is this source's coinage, not a recognized conformance level — see
[[iso-42001|ISO/IEC 42001]]. The $47,200 penalty figure has no jurisdiction or method attached.
