## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**A secondary source.** The figures come from Netskope and IBM; the playbook cites the LinkedIn post
relaying them rather than either primary report — both of which are public, and both of which
Appendix B recommends ingesting.

**The playbook omits the probability.** "20% of organizations reported a breach due to Shadow AI" is
arguably the most decision-relevant number here — it's what converts the +$670K severity into an
expected value. It's not in the playbook.

The **6× year-over-year growth** is also dropped, and it's what makes any baseline set today
obsolete within a year.

Netskope sells the CASB tooling its own data recommends, and is named in the Discover step.
IBM sells AI governance tooling and appears in the Premium governance tier. The +$670K is a
correlation across environments, not a measured causal effect.
