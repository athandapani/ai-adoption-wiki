## Body

**Curriculum:** 4–8 hours of contact time across 2–4 sessions, live cohorts of 15–25.

Topics: foundations of generative AI (non-mathematical); practical prompting; "Hallucinations,
bias, data protection, intellectual property, and your organisation's specific acceptable use
policy"; hands-on practice on the learner's own real work; a use-case map of 3–5 job-specific
applications per learner.

The rule the playbook elevates, correctly: **train leaders first or in parallel — never last.**

The failure pattern it describes when literacy is skipped: "15% of staff use it daily, 30% have
logged in once, and the rest haven't touched it".

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**The 4–8 hour prescription has no evidence behind it.** No comparison against shorter or longer
formats, no measured outcome, no sample. It's a training vendor's course length presented as a
finding, by a company that sells training.

The "15% daily / 30% once / rest never" pattern is offered as an argument for literacy training. It
is equally an argument that the tool didn't fit the work — an explanation the source, selling
training, has no incentive to consider.

Note the internal inconsistency this creates in the playbook: Section 8.5 recommends **4–8 hours**
of AI literacy, while Section 4.5 and the [[ninety-day-governance-launch|90-day plan]] specify a
**15-minute** shadow AI awareness session. Both are described as mandatory, neither references the
other, and it's unclear whether the 15 minutes is inside or outside the 4–8 hours.
