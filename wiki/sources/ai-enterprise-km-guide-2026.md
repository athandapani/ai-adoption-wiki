## Body

Pricing (all seven match the playbook's Section 10.1 table):

| Tool | Price |
|---|---|
| Glean | "$40-100/user/yr ($100K-2M)" |
| Guru | "$15-30/user/yr" |
| Notion AI | "Business $20 + AI $10/user" |
| Slab | "$8-15/user/yr" |
| Confluence AI | "$5.75-11/user + AI" |
| Microsoft Viva Topics | "$30/user/mo" |

**Recommended stacks:** startup (<50) "Notion AI + Slack AI + Slite ~$20K/yr"; mid-market (50–500)
"Glean + Notion + Guru ~$150K/yr"; enterprise B2B SaaS "Glean + Slab + GoLinks + Stack Overflow";
Fortune 500/Microsoft "Viva Topics + M365 Copilot + SharePoint + Confluence AI".

Its claims: AI-powered KM reduces information-search time by "65%" and boosts productivity "30%",
addressing the **"1.8 hours/day"** employees spend locating information. Market to "$45B by 2030".

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**The 1.8 hours/day figure contradicts ref [^10]'s 2.5 hours/day.** Both are cited in the playbook.
Section 2.4 builds its $1M–$2M recovered-capacity claim on **2.5** and never mentions **1.8** — a
~39% difference feeding straight into the headline ROI. Two of its own sources disagree by nearly
40% on the single input to its biggest number.

**The recommended stacks don't match either.** The playbook's "Notion AI + Guru + Slack AI:
~$100–150K/year" for $50M–$150M firms isn't here; this source's startup stack is "~$20K/yr" — an
order of magnitude apart. Only the mid-market row matches.

Affiliate-style comparison site, unbylined ("AIpedia Editorial Team"). The 65%/30% figures have no
methodology.
