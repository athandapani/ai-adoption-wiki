## Body

Four causes of 2024's AI difficulties:

- **Data infrastructure** — legacy systems struggled with AI requirements; "data quality
  complications plagued rollouts"
- **Talent shortage** — "Companies fought over a limited pool of qualified professionals, driving
  up costs"
- **Regulation** — the EU AI Act "forced many organizations to halt initiatives while navigating
  compliance demands"
- **ROI** — "scaling these successes across the enterprise proved elusive"

Adoption context: "about 75% of companies implementing some form of generative AI by year's end".

Its forecast: 2025 would bring pragmatic, targeted implementations with measurable outcomes and
human-AI collaboration focused on augmentation over replacement.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**Status `dormant`.** This is a January 2025 retrospective on 2024, cited in a 2026 playbook. Every
claim it makes is measured more recently and more precisely elsewhere in this vault — refs [^1],
