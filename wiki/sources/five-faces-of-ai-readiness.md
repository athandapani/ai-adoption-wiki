## Body

Captured via **Playwright** (WebFetch returned HTTP 403).

**The five archetypes, verbatim:** AI enthusiasts (innovation-driven, channel as champions); AI
curious (pragmatic, need questions answered — use sandbox hackathons); AI cautious (**data
security, ethical guardrails, harm to collaboration** — mandate human-in-the-loop); AI sceptics
(**demand empirical proof** — use transparent A/B testing); AI opposed (**job displacement and
surveillance fears** — address directly, e.g. a "Pro-Human AI Charter").

**Frontstage / backstage resistance** — the study's actual contribution:

> "In frontstage settings – public, visible areas – employees may appear to comply with AI mandates
> to manage impressions. However, in backstage settings, they often engage in informal critique,
> 'clowning' AI tools, or finding ways to circumvent and delay their use."

> "Even 'AI enthusiasts' tasked with implementing strategies... often privately doubt their
> effectiveness."

Also: "62% of adults now interact with AI several times a week" (US); private AI investment
"estimated at $660 billion in 2026"; the **"capability overhang"** between what AI can do and how
it's used.

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**The playbook renames all five archetypes and mismaps two.** Its "Anxious" (job displacement) is
this source's **opposed**; its "Skeptics" (doubt reliability) is this source's **sceptics**, not its
"cautious" — and the **cautious** category (data security, ethics) vanishes entirely. Interventions
land on the wrong archetypes. See [[five-human-postures-toward-ai|Five Human Postures Toward AI]].

It's also dated "May 2026" in the playbook; published **1 June 2026**.

**The dropped finding is the costly one.** Frontstage/backstage resistance is the direct mechanism
behind [[shadow-ai|shadow AI]] — 47% on personal accounts is backstage resistance with a network
signature — and it undercuts
[[leadership-modeling-imperative|the leadership-modeling prescription]], since managers performing
adoption is exactly the frontstage behavior identified as the failure mode.

Method caveat: sentiment analysis of **public social media** measures the frontstage the study warns
is unreliable.
