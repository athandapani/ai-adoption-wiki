## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**The 3.4%-of-revenue figure breaks the playbook's own arithmetic, and it doesn't notice.**

If average SMB IT spend is **3.4% of revenue**, and BCG's benchmark says AI should be **1.7% of
revenue**, then AI is **half the entire IT budget**. The same Section 6.4 says "15–35% [of IT
budget] is aligned with mid-market practice" and warns that "above 40% requires rigorous
per-initiative ROI discipline". The playbook's own recommended benchmark lands at ~50% — above its
own red line — and it presents both figures on the same page without reconciling them.

The most likely resolution is that the BCG 1.7% is aspirational planning data from large
enterprises while the 3.4% is observed SMB actuals, and the two populations shouldn't be combined.
That's exactly the reconciliation the playbook needed to do and didn't.
