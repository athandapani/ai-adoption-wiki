## Body

**Author:** Damien Clothier.

**Five weighted factors:** Urgency (25%), Technical Complexity (20%), Internal Capability (20%),
Budget Constraints (20%), Strategic Importance (15%).

**Three delivery models compared over 18 months:**

| Model | Cost |
|---|---|
| In-house | $200K–$450K |
| Big 4 consulting | $500K–$2M+ |
| Mid-market consulting | $35K–$250K |

Conclusion: mid-market consulting delivers "fastest time-to-value (production deployment by month
3, ROI by month 4-6)" at "lowest risk ($50K-$100K investment, 3-6 month commitment)".

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**The playbook's Section 3.4 build-vs-buy table is not in this source.** That table — six rows
covering time to value, customization, data sovereignty, long-term TCO, required IT capability, and
IP risk, comparing "Buy (SaaS)" against "Build (Custom/API-First)" — appears nowhere here.

This source answers a different question: **who should do the work** (your team, a Big 4 firm, or a
mid-market consultancy), not **whether to build or buy**. The playbook attaches its own table to a
framework about delivery models.

Also self-serving in an obvious way: a mid-market AI consultancy publishes a weighted framework
concluding that mid-market consultancies are the best option, at costs an order of magnitude below
the Big 4 alternative it names.

Status `revisiting`: captured cleanly, but the playbook's use of it doesn't survive checking.
