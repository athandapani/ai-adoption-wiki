## Body

The playbook's Section 2.4 ROI table is built almost entirely on this source:

- "enterprise-wide RAG implementation typically delivers a **300-500% return on investment within
  the first year alone**"
- Knowledge workers spend approximately **"2.5 hours daily"** searching for information
- "productivity gains of **30-50%**", recovering "45-75 minutes per employee daily"
- 1,000 knowledge workers → "**$5-8 million annually** in recovered productive time"
- Decision quality: information gaps reduced "**40-60%** for critical decisions"
- Knowledge preservation: "**70%** of previously ephemeral knowledge" retained from departing
  senior employees
- Compliance risk exposure reduced "25-35%"

## Counter-arguments & Data Gaps

**The $300,000-per-departing-senior-employee figure is not in this source.** The playbook attributes
it here, in Section 2.4 and again in 7.4. It's the number that makes the Second Brain business case
close inside a year, and the capture doesn't contain it. The **70% preservation** figure is real;
the dollar value it gets multiplied against is not sourced. See
[[knowledge-attrition-cost|The Cost of Knowledge Attrition]].

**The 2.5 hours/day figure is contradicted by another source the playbook cites.** Ref [^40] says
**1.8 hours/day**. The playbook uses 2.5 to derive its $1M–$2M recovered-capacity claim and never
mentions the smaller figure — a ~39% swing straight into the headline.

**No methodology anywhere.** Stratechi is a management consultancy; this is a marketing page whose
title contains its own conclusion ("| 300-500% ROI"). No sample, no method, no citations, no author.
Every figure is a round range.

Status `revisiting`: these numbers carry more weight in the playbook than any others, and they are
the least supported in the corpus.
